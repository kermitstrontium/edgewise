Edgewise

One line through every square.
Finish on the edge, and the map grows.

How to play

  1. Draw one line from 1 through every square, passing the numbers in order.
  2. Thick bars are walls. The line can't cross them.
  3. The last number always sits on the edge. Finish there and the next grid opens on that side. Your line carries straight on into it.
  4. Drag back over your line to undo. Tap any square of it to rewind to there.
  5. Map zooms out to show the whole journey.
